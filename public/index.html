Our philosophy

Before you throw it away, give it one more chance!

A ₹200 mouse with a worn-out scroll wheel. A robot toy with a loose wire. A small gadget with one damaged component. Most people simply buy a new one — but often the real problem is just one small part.

At MALVITHA we look for that small fault: a loose connection, a faulty switch, a damaged wire, a worn encoder or a tiny circuit issue. Where it is technically and economically practical, we try to restore the device instead of letting it become waste.

Honest assessment, always. Not every device can be repaired. We inspect each item and tell you plainly whether a repair is possible and worthwhile.

  • Reduce e-wasteFewer working parts thrown into landfill.
  • Responsible useRepair first, replace only when needed.
  • Small faults firstFind the one part that stopped everything.
  • Low-cost items tooCheap gadgets deserve a second chance.

Think repair. Reduce waste. Give it a second life.
